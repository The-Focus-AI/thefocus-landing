Early in the twenty-first century, the frontier labs advanced machine intelligence into its operational phase — a resource to be produced, routed, governed, and consumed at scale.

Intelligence became abundant before organizations learned how to absorb it. Pilots that dazzled in the demo dissolved in production. Workers saved time that no ledger could find.

The companies that pulled ahead were the ones that could make themselves legible to machines: that wrote down what they knew, verified what they built, and decided what the freed capacity was for.

This was not called digital transformation. It was called the Org Age.
