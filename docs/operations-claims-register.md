# Website claim boundaries

This concept makes the following distinctions explicit:

- Monthly pricing is the proposed engagement model, subject to a defined scope and team allocation.
- Reliability means an agreed operating path, verification, visible exceptions, and ownership. It is not a guarantee that failures never occur.
- Fountain Creek's next rebuild is proposed; earlier integration work is distinguished from it.
- Steering House's verified models do not establish a measured reduction in overruns.
- The anonymized media work is delivered with UpperHand AI. The larger replacement is not described as complete.
- The current 133-page working edition is available in the owner-only preview. Its next editorial revision remains in progress.
- Enquiry and book-signup forms are demonstrations, not successful submissions or subscriptions. The private preview PDF download itself works.

No invented testimonials, performance figures, current company revenue, or realized savings have been added. Internal source records and commercial review notes remain in the private book repository.
